1. Introduction & Legal Basis

Welcome to VoyageAI ("we", "our", "us"). We provide an artificial intelligence-driven luxury travel concierge that assists users worldwide in discovering travel itineraries, evaluating real-time 2026 airfare benchmarks, handpicking accommodations, and planning bespoke holidays.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal and behavioral information is collected, stored, processed, and safeguarded across all VoyageAI platforms. By engaging with our website or querying our AI travel assistant, you acknowledge the practices described herein.

Our Core Privacy Commitment: VoyageAI never monetizes or sells your personal information, travel queries, or chat logs to third-party ad networks or data brokers. All recommendations are generated solely for your itinerary.

2. Google Gemini 3.8 Flash AI Architecture & Data Ethics

VoyageAI uses Google’s state-of-the-art Gemini 3.8 Flash foundation model to power its conversational trip planning and live price retrieval. Because our agent handles natural language travel inquiries, we adhere to strict AI data governance principles:

Gemini 3.8 Flash Intelligence Engine: Queries submitted in the chat interface are processed via high-performance Google AI API channels. Sessions are ephemeral and encrypted in memory during execution.

  • Zero Model Training On Private Logs: We do not authorize or allow your personal chat messages or travel dates to be used by Google or VoyageAI to train general public generative models.
  • Live Real-Time Web Search Grounding: Gemini 3.8 Flash verifies real-world 2026 airline prices and destination guidelines using Google Search Grounding to guarantee fresh, factual figures.
  • Client-Side Session Isolation: Multi-turn conversation state resides in your browser's local memory (`sessionStorage`) and can be flushed completely at any moment using the "New Trip" button.
  • Google AI Studio Governance: API interactions comply strictly with Google's API Terms and Enterprise Privacy Safeguards. You may review Google's official privacy policies at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Information We Collect

We restrict data collection to what is essential to engineer your personalized travel itineraries and ensure smooth web operations:

Contact Details

Name, email address, or phone number submitted voluntarily through direct contact inquiries or support channels.

Trip Preferences

Target destinations (Europe, Dubai, US, Egypt), party composition (solo, honeymoon, family), duration, dates, and budget estimates.

Technical Telemetry

Browser user agent, IP address, device type, display resolution, and theme selection (`dark` or `light`) for responsive rendering.

Client API Tokens (Optional)

If you enter your own Google Gemini Studio API key, it is saved securely in your browser's `localStorage` and never transmitted to external third parties.

4. Purposes of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively under the following lawful bases and operational purposes:

  • Itinerary Generation: Synthesizing customized day-by-day schedules with dining, cultural excursions, and flight advice tailored to your budget.
  • Real-Time Price Discovery: Comparing roundtrip flight options from major carriers (e.g. Emirates, Delta, Lufthansa, EgyptAir) to ensure cost transparency.
  • Service Security & Health Monitoring: Preventing malicious traffic spikes, enforcing rate-limiting, and maintaining 99.9% uptime.
  • UI/UX Personalization: Remembering your Dark/Light mode visual preference and active consultation states across page transitions.

5. Data Security, Cloud Infrastructure & Encryption

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction of client information:

  • 256-Bit TLS In-Transit Encryption: Every byte transferred between your browser, our Node.js backend server, and the Google Gemini API is encrypted via modern HTTPS/TLS protocols.
  • Sanitized Memory Footprint: Server-side in-memory chat payloads are immediately released upon completion of response streaming.
  • Strict Access Controls: Server infrastructure and development keys are isolated with multi-factor authentication and role-based access.

7. Cookies & Local Browser Storage

VoyageAI adopts a minimalist, privacy-first cookie policy. We do not employ third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral profiling pixels.

  • `voyageai_theme`: Stored in `localStorage` to preserve your Dark or Light mode preference across visits without flickering.
  • `voyageai_gemini_key`: Stored optionally in `localStorage` only if you choose to persist your personal Google AI Studio API key.
  • Full Browser Control: You may inspect, clear, or block all stored keys via your browser settings at any time without impacting basic browsing.

8. Your Rights (GDPR, UK-GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Regardless of your geographic location, VoyageAI affords you full autonomy over your data under the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

Right to Access

Request copies of any personal records or inquiries associated with your contact profile.

Right to Rectify

Request immediate correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete records.

Right to Erasure

Request permanent deletion of your data under the "Right to be Forgotten".

Right to Restrict

Exercise your privacy rights freely without facing degradation in service quality.

9. Children's Privacy & Cross-Border Data Transfers

Protection of Minors: VoyageAI is designed for adults and families planning travel. We do not intentionally collect data from individuals under 16 years of age. If you believe a minor has shared personal credentials, please notify us for immediate purge.

Cross-Border Transfers: To compute real-time flight rates and execute Google Gemini 3.8 Flash generation, data may be processed on secure servers located in the European Union and the United States under Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
